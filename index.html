What Is Gavel Score?

  • A higher gavel score = a more profitable gavel.

  • Gavel score measures how profitable a gavel is by multiplying its mutations by their percentages.

  • Enter your gavel information and then click calculate to see your gavel scores.

  • Compare up to 5 gavels at a time by adding additional slots.
